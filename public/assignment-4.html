Timing assumptions

Both runs use arrivals [0, 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 11, 14] and service durations [5, 6, 4, 7, 5, 6, 4, 5] in minutes. G1 and G2 are initially occupied until minute 4 and 6, respectively. FCFS assigns the first eligible truck. A tie between gates goes to G1.

Traditional trucks are eligible upon arrival and travel for one minute after the call. SmartMarsh trucks reach staging one minute after arrival, acknowledge after 0.5 minute with engines off, prepare for 0.5 minute with engines on, and travel for two minutes.
